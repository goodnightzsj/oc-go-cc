## Design Context

### Users

Developers and operators running routatic-proxy locally or on a small server. They use the console repeatedly to confirm proxy health, inspect individual requests, understand token and cost trends, and diagnose routing or provider failures without leaving their coding workflow.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a focused operational instrument: technically capable without looking noisy, playful, or promotional.

### Aesthetic Direction

Retain the existing compact dashboard, indigo accent, embedded HTML/CSS/JavaScript stack, and light/dark compatibility. Prefer clear data hierarchy, legible tables, restrained SVG charts, and visible system state. Avoid decorative gradients, card proliferation, dense animation, and generic SaaS marketing patterns.

### Design Principles

1. Make token, cost, latency, and failure definitions explicit and consistent across every view.
2. Optimize for fast scanning first, then progressive detail through keyboard-accessible interactions.
3. Use color to encode state and series, never as the only carrier of meaning.
4. Preserve focus, reduced-motion support, and readable contrast as baseline behavior.
5. Reuse the existing zero-dependency components before adding markup or JavaScript.
